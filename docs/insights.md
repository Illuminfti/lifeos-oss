# Derived insight layer

Insights combine canonical claims with structured noncanonical observations. They are generated records, not truth sources.

Every insight includes:

```yaml
insight_id: ins_...
algorithm: relationship-radar/v2
generated_at: ...
canon_revision_hash: ...
input_claim_ids: [...]
input_observation_ids: [...]
confidence: 0.0..1.0
limitations: [...]
```

Promoting an insight requires the normal owner-reviewed path into a concept, decision, or claim.

## Relationship radar

Facets include recorded relation claims, unresolved linked commitments, disputed relations, meaningful-contact cadence when observations exist, and change from the relationship's own baseline.

The system may say that contact fell below a recorded baseline while two promises remain open. It must not declare a relationship healthy, unhealthy, good, or bad from one opaque score.

## Self-pattern laboratory

The reference implementation aligns numeric observations by day and reports Pearson association only when a minimum sample exists. Its language is explicitly associational. Future implementations may add lagged models, within-person baselines, confidence intervals, and robust handling of travel or missing data.

It must never turn correlation into causal diagnosis.

## Life-function coverage

Each canonical life function can carry owner-defined cadence, minimum meaningful actions, importance, and neglect-window claims. The derived view shows linked projects, assets, events, decisions, and open loops. It does not invent a universal ideal balance.

## Circumstance change feed

The feed is computed from canonical claim deltas: new constraints, changed statuses, blocked or unblocked projects, relation changes, available or risky assets, contradicted assumptions, and closing windows.

## Decision and outcome ledger

The view connects a decision to intended outcomes, created commitments, actions, observations, superseding choices, and measured outcomes. Missing outcome evidence is not interpreted as failure.

## Leverage map

The graph projection can highlight subjects connected to many active projects or life functions, assets that unlock several constraints, and actions capable of closing several loops. Connectivity is a prompt for attention, not proof of causal power or human worth.
